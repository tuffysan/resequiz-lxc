# Resequiz 12.1 – Question Quality Overhaul

Version 12.1 deliberately reduces the question count to improve real quiz quality.

## What changed

The full bank was reviewed structurally and by provenance/template pattern. The two bulk expansion generations that introduced the majority of weak distractors and repeated formula questions were treated conservatively:

- The v7.2 paired/template expansion (`q10501`–`q11217`) was removed. It contained useful facts, but too many items used unrelated words as distractors and often asked the same fact twice.
- Mechanical v7.5 series were removed, including repeated arithmetic, unit conversions, BPM calculations, century calculations, football score arithmetic, basketball arithmetic and repeated audio questions.
- High-value v7.5 material was retained: local image questions, coherent general knowledge, selected animal classification, film/TV, food, music, trivia, travel, sport and Sweden facts.
- The retained Sweden set received new semantically plausible distractors.
- v7.3 local flag and music media questions remain.
- The core bank remains unchanged except for earlier exact-duplicate cleanup.

## Result

- Before: 13,002 questions
- After: 10,614 questions
- Removed: 2,388 questions
- Unique IDs: 10,614
- Duplicate options: 0

The lower number is intentional. Resequiz now favours believable answer alternatives and useful variety over a larger headline count.

## Quality rule going forward

New questions must not be added merely to increase the bank size. A question should be accepted only when the prompt is natural Swedish, the fact is defensible, wrong answers are plausible and grammatically compatible, and the item is meaningfully different from existing questions.
